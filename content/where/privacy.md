Your diary stays on your Mac and is not uploaded to us. Qooee Where and these web pages do not use advertising or analytics.

## Your records

The app stores the information needed for the features you use:

- Work hours, breaks, notes and checking and correction history.
- Optional home/away and activity recordings.
- Your Wi-Fi and display rules, preferences and recovery copies.

Optional home detection uses the Wi-Fi networks and displays you choose. macOS requires Location permission for Wi-Fi recognition; the app does not request geographic coordinates. Optional activity recording records periods of activity, without recording what you type or view. You can switch these features off in Settings.

## Storage and sharing

Local files rely on your Mac’s security settings; the app does not add separate encryption. You choose where to save exports and backups, and whether to share them or use a synchronised folder.

## Retention and deletion

Your records remain until you remove them. Corrections and ordinary deletion can leave earlier versions and recovery copies. **Permanently remove…** also removes selected recordings from the app’s recovery and import copies. Exported files and other backups must be deleted separately. Deleting presence recordings does not delete separately recorded work hours.

Uninstalling the app may leave its data behind. To remove all local data, open the records folder from **Backup & recovery**, quit the app, then remove its records and settings files. See [Support](../support/) for help.

## Support and website visits

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments. We use these to answer you, resolve issues and handle privacy requests. We retain correspondence only as long as needed for those purposes or legal obligations, then delete or de-identify it.

Website visits provide connection information, such as an IP address, to the service providers that deliver and protect these pages. We use service providers to operate the website and support email; they may process information outside Australia. We may also disclose information where required by law.

## Apple services

Apple handles App Store purchases, downloads and updates under [Apple’s privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/au/legal/privacy/). We may receive sales reports from Apple, and diagnostics if your Apple settings allow sharing them.

## Privacy requests and complaints

Manage your diary in the app. For access, correction or deletion of personal information you have sent us, or to make a privacy complaint, email [hello@qooee.net](mailto:hello@qooee.net?subject=Qooee%20Where%20privacy). We aim to respond within 30 days. If unresolved, the [Office of the Australian Information Commissioner](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-complaints) explains available complaint options.

We update this policy and its date when our privacy practices change.
