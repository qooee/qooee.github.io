This policy applies to **Qooee Where for Mac**, provided by **evil robots pty ltd** (ABN 21 667 313 911), PO Box 201, Berowra Heights NSW 2082, Australia. For privacy enquiries, contact [hello@qooee.net](mailto:hello@qooee.net?subject=Qooee%20Where%20privacy).

Qooee Where is a personal working-from-home hours log. You do not need a Qooee account. Your diary is stored on your Mac; the app does not upload it to us, and we cannot remotely read it. It does not connect to Qooee Contacts. Other Qooee products have their own policies.

## Information kept on your Mac

The app keeps the information needed for the features you use:

- **WFH and other sessions:** dates, start/finish times, work location, breaks, optional notes, work/personal classification, suggested or confirmed status, recording time zones and original entry versions.
- **Checking history:** review markers, checked No WFH days, your log’s start date and any earlier-record reference you add.
- **Detected presence:** Home, Not home or uncertain periods, their times, whether they came from automatic detection or a temporary choice, and personal classifications you apply.
- **Optional activity intervals:** coarse start/end times, detected location and time zone. Activity recording starts switched off. It does not retain input content.
- **Detection rules:** optional labels, saved Wi-Fi network names and trusted access-point addresses, display vendor/model/serial identifiers and available product names, enabled rules, and temporary choices with expiry times.
- **Preferences and recovery:** report settings, recording choices, interface preferences, backup receipts, recovery snapshots and retained import/restoration copies.

Local identifiers, revisions and change information help preserve relationships, detect conflicting saves and retain correction history. They are not advertising identifiers and are not sent to us.

## Optional detection and permissions

Home detection starts switched off. You choose which rules to save and enable. Wi-Fi recognition reads the connected network’s name and, where available, its access-point address. macOS requires Location permission for that information. The app requests it only when you choose **Allow Wi-Fi recognition…**.

Qooee Where does not request geographic coordinates, scan nearby networks or build a geographic travel history. Display recognition reads connected external-display metadata, not screen contents, and does not require Location permission. Raw signal observations are used in memory; the timeline keeps the resulting presence periods rather than a continuous history of network or display identifiers. Rules you explicitly save remain in local settings.

Activity recording, if enabled, checks elapsed time since keyboard or mouse input. It does not install an input event tap or store typed text, click positions, app names or window contents. Its intervals remain observations to review. The work timer is a separate choice and can include idle time while the Mac is awake. Sleep, inactive-session and closed-app time are not reconstructed.

You can use the log manually without Location permission. No camera, microphone, contacts, calendar or screen-recording permission is requested. macOS file panels grant access to the files or folders you select.

## What we do not receive

The app sends us no diary entries, notes, presence periods, activity intervals, network names, access-point addresses or display identifiers. There is no diary server, remote employee dashboard, advertising, tracking, in-app analytics, third-party crash-reporting SDK or AI integration.

We do not sell or rent your diary, use it for advertising or profiling, or train AI models on it. We see diary information only if you separately choose to send it, for example as an attachment in a support email.

## Local storage and security

Diary, rule and recovery files are readable JSON in the app’s local macOS storage. The app uses atomic, coordinated writes and conflict checks, but these do not replace backups. It does not add its own encryption or password. Your Mac account, file permissions and any disk encryption you use protect access. Other people or software with sufficient access to your files may be able to read them.

Your device-management, backup or file-sync arrangements may copy the files. An export saved in a synchronised folder may be uploaded by your chosen service under its own terms. Qooee Where itself has no cloud or direct device sync.

## Exports and independent backups

You choose where exports are saved and whether to share them. Annual packages contain a readable report, CSV files and a complete diary. Report notes are optional. Complete diary and backup JSON include notes, breaks, checking history, original entry versions, presence, activity and personal classifications.

Independent backups also retain report settings and log coverage. They do not include device-specific home rules or macOS permissions. Restoring into an empty installation leaves recording off until you set it up. The app keeps a local backup receipt with the saved date and path; this is not sent to us.

Exports are separate copies. Editing or deleting an app record does not change an earlier export, and the app does not automatically send reports to an accountant or to us. If you share a file, the recipient controls their copy.

## Retention and deletion

Your active diary and detection rules remain until you change or remove them. There is no hosted diary account to delete and no server-side diary retention period. Support ending or a new edition appearing does not cause the app to delete your records.

The app retains up to 20 recovery snapshots made before diary edits. Automatic presence/activity checkpoints do not rotate those snapshots. Original import copies and files preserved during restoration are retained separately.

Normal deletion removes selected recordings from the active diary and offers Undo until the next review action or app restart. Earlier copies may still contain them. **Permanently remove…** also removes selected recordings from app-held recovery and import copies and cannot be undone. If a retained copy cannot be read or changed, the app reports a failure rather than claiming removal. Your exports, Trash, system snapshots and independent backups remain outside that operation.

Deleting observed presence does not remove work sessions recorded separately from it. Removing a detection rule changes future detection, without rewriting past records.

To remove all local records, first export anything you want to keep. Locate the records folder through **Backup & recovery → Show records and recovery files in Finder**, quit the app, then remove the diary, rule, preference, Recovery and Imports files you no longer want. Manage exported files, Trash and backups separately. Removing the app alone may leave its records behind. We cannot remotely erase files on your Mac or copies you shared.

## Your controls

You can inspect, edit, classify and check your hours; keep a manual log; enable or disable each recording feature; add or remove rules; export records; and restore supported backups. Explicit corrections preserve earlier versions for review. Longer grouped gaps remain excluded; matching WFH gaps of up to one minute count as work, while explicit breaks remain excluded.

Return temporary Home/Not home choices to **Automatic home detection** and switch off detection to stop presence recording. Revoke Wi-Fi Location access in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services** if desired; display rules and manual entries remain available. Work and activity recording have separate controls. See [Support](../support/) for instructions.

## When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your address, message and any details or attachments you choose to include. Our support email service is operated in Australia. Your own email provider may process your message elsewhere under its own terms.

We use correspondence to answer questions, investigate issues, handle privacy requests or complaints, and keep records needed for those purposes or legal obligations. We do not add you to marketing emails because you asked for help. We retain correspondence only while needed for these purposes, then delete or de-identify it. We may disclose information we actually hold where the law requires it; we do not have a hosted diary database to search.

Please send only what is needed. A fictional example or redacted screenshot is usually sufficient. Do not send passwords or a complete diary for a general question, and check attachments for private information before sending them.

The app does not attach diary content to analytics or diagnostic reports. See [Apple’s privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/au/legal/privacy/).

These product, support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting them involves standard web requests to GitHub, which may record information such as your IP address for security. These pages do not add analytics, advertising cookies, external fonts or embedded third-party media. This describes the Qooee Where pages; other websites you choose to visit have their own practices.
