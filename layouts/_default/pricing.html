The first release

Contact sharing, with more card and sync options in Pro

Free covers everyday contact sharing.

Create one profile card, add or import contacts, share by link or QR code, and review recent contact changes.

Pro adds card views, audiences, and device sync.

Choose visible fields for each card view, assign a whole card to a connection or saved audience, and sync with Apple Contacts while the app is active or when you choose Sync now.

The create and delete preferences apply globally to linked entries on this device.

Planned separately

Outside the first release

Contact export, independent per-contact field visibility, fuller change history, merge/conflict review, and priority support are not available in the first release. They are not included in the launch Free or Pro plans.

We will describe these features when they are ready. No launch dates have been announced.

FAQ

Short answers, up front

Do I need to pay to use Qooee Contacts?

No. The core product is free to use.

Can I import my existing contacts for free?

Yes. Importing contacts is included in the free plan.

Can I export my data if I stop using Qooee?

Contact export is not available in the first release. It is planned separately.

What does pro add?

Pro adds multiple card views, field visibility per card, saved audiences, and Apple Contacts sync on this device with global create/delete preferences.
