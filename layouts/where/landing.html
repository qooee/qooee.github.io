A few practical questions

Before you begin.

Is Qooee Where available yet?

Qooee Where is being prepared for the Mac App Store. We’ll add the Store link here when it is available.

Do I have to use automatic detection?

No. You can enter your actual WFH hours yourself. Home detection and activity recording are optional, and manual use does not require Location permission.

Does it calculate my tax deduction?

Qooee Where records hours and produces reports. It does not calculate a deduction or determine your eligibility. Keep any expense evidence you need separately and check the current ATO guidance for your circumstances.

Can I use it without the internet?

Yes. Recording, checking and exporting your diary work offline. Visiting these pages, emailing support, and downloading or updating the app use separate online services.
